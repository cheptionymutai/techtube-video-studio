Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Techtube Video Studio Ltd, your privacy is a core part of our commitment to transparency. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our StratBot strategy builder or contact us through our website.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to build your strategy and send your quote: your name, email and business details.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us build a bespoke video marketing strategy for you, including:

  • Identity Data: Your name and email address.
  • Business Data: Industry type, website URL, and business/product descriptions.
  • Strategic Data: Content preferences, video format, and production frequency.
  • Technical Data: IP address, approximate location, and device and browser information. We use your location to show quotes in the right currency, and our analytics and advertising tools (see section 7) also receive this data.
  • Payment Data: When you pay a deposit, your payment details are handled directly by Paystack. We receive confirmation of the payment but do not store your card or M-Pesa details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to fuel our Hybrid Production Model. Specifically:

  • To generate the custom video strategy via our AI engine.
  • To deliver official quotations and invoices to your inbox.
  • To address you by name in our communications.
  • To trigger automated follow-ups ensuring you received your strategy details.
  • To analyze industry trends to improve StratBot's accuracy.
  • To email you updates about our products and services. You can opt out at any time by emailing hello@techtubestudio.com.

3. AI Processing

When you provide a URL or business description, this data is processed by Google Gemini AI to create your roadmap. This data is used only for the generation of your specific strategy and is not used by Techtube to "train" public models.

4. Data Storage and Security

We host our infrastructure on Google Cloud (Firebase & Cloud Run), which employs enterprise-grade security. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party marketers.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as we are doing business with you, and until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion, email hello@techtubestudio.com.

Where the law requires us to keep certain records, such as invoices and payment records, we will keep only what is required.

6. Third-Party Services

To deliver our service, we use trusted sub-processors and tools:

  • Google Cloud: For hosting and AI processing.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail): For email delivery of your strategies, quotes and invoices.
  • Paystack: For processing deposit and invoice payments by card or M-Pesa.
  • ipapi.co: To detect your country from your IP address so we can show prices in the right currency.
  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager: For website analytics.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel: For measuring and improving our advertising.
  • YouTube: For videos embedded on our pages.

7. Analytics, Cookies and Advertising

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use our website, and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies, and may collect your IP address, device and browser details, and the pages you view. Videos embedded on our pages are served by YouTube, which may also set cookies when you play a video.

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline these cookies, and you can change your choice at any time using the button below. You can also limit or block cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad personalization in your Google and Meta account settings.

8. Your Rights

Under Kenyan Data Protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your data at any time. To do so, please contact us at the email address below.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your data, please reach out to us:
